— Directions for use

Four steps to a healthier routine.

01

Initial Familiarization

Allow the animal to become familiar with the spray by introducing it gradually during routine handling.

02

Diluted Introduction

For initial use, mix 5 to 10 sprays per 100mL of water to facilitate progressive adaptation.

03

Direct Application

Apply 3 or more sprays evenly across the teeth, gums, and oral cavity.

04

Progressive Application

For sensitive or untrained animals, continue diluted use while gradually transitioning to direct oral application.
